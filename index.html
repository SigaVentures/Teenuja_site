About

Model, dancer, artist

I’m Teenooja, 28, born and based in Chennai. I hold an M.Sc., I draw and I dance, and I bring both of those into how I work on set — patient with direction, quick with expression, comfortable holding a pose until the frame is right.

I’m comfortable in Indian, semi-western and western wear, indoors and outdoors, and I have no cosmetic or dust allergies. I’m a fresher to commercial work, which means I come in without habits and listen closely to the brief.
