Frequently asked

What kind of work do you take on?

Print and catalogue shoots, designer and bridal shoots, calendar work and acting. I’m comfortable in ethnic, semi-western and western wear, indoors or outdoors.

Where are you based, and do you travel?

Chennai. I shoot across Tamil Nadu and am happy to travel for the right project, with travel and stay arranged.

What are your measurements?

Height 5'5", weight 50 kg, 32 / 28 / 34, shoe size 7–8. Complexion light brown, hair and eyes black.

Are you represented by an agency?

Not at present — I take bookings directly. Reach me on +91 99447 30755 or at teenooja97@gmail.com.

Any allergies or restrictions?

No reported allergies to dust or cosmetics. Shorts and bold scenes are not part of my comfort list.
