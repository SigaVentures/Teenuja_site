About

Teenooja

I’m a model and performing artist from Chennai. I came to this work through dance and drawing — two things that taught me how a body reads in a frame and how much a small change of line or expression can shift a photograph.

I’m 28, I hold an M.Sc., and I speak Tamil and English. On set I’m calm, I take direction closely, and I’d rather give a photographer twenty honest variations than one rehearsed pose. I’m a fresher to commercial work, so I come without habits to unlearn.

My portfolio runs from full bridal silk and temple jewellery through earth-tone traditional sarees to denim and knitwear on the street. Ethnic, semi-western and western wear are all comfortable ground, indoors and outdoors alike.
